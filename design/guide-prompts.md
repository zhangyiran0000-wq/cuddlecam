## 独立接吻样张

Use case: style-transfer. Edit target is the supplied reference photograph. Produce a literally transparent camera pose overlay of just the two adults. Exact tracing registration: source edges, camera crop, relative scale, eye and nose and lip positions, silhouettes, shoulders, fingers and wrist must stay at precisely the same pixel-normalized locations. Do not reframe or recompose. Art direction: a master romance manga illustrator's delicate brush pen contour, tender and restrained, long sweeping tapered ivory-white strokes, confident heavier outer arcs and very fine finer lines only for eyelids, noses, mouths, contact hands, neckline. 3 to 5 beautiful gestural locks per head, no dense hair strands. Calm uninked negative space, elegant overlapping profiles and anatomically correct fingers. White-ink drawing on completely transparent alpha throughout: interior faces, hair, clothing and background all transparent, no fills, no scene, no hatching, no texture, no shadows or halo, no type, no frame. This is a shooting template, not a finished portrait, so preserve exact source positions. Output one square image matching the square reference exactly.

## 姿势图册

Use case: style-transfer. Edit this EXACT 3 by 3 photographic contact sheet into a production pose-guide LINE ART ATLAS. Nine distinct squares in matching row-major order. Trace every cell's two adults in the same framing and size, with the same crop, face directions, kiss contacts, shoulder lines, and hand locations. The nine equal cell boundaries remain exactly at thirds with no borders and no gaps. Use the visual language of an elegant romance manga line illustrator: dark black liquid ink, beautifully curved tapered strokes, selective thick-to-thin silhouettes, three broad flowing locks per head, a delicate lid and nose, a concise lip contour, a little ear and neckline, distinct graceful fingers when visible. Eliminate all loose hatching, shadows, textile grain, individual strands, background edges, highlights, and line clutter. Very sparse, beautiful effortless ink calligraphy. Transparent negative space outside AND INSIDE both figures, clear alpha, ink strokes only, no white fills, no opaque skin or clothes, no text, no frames. Use ONLY dark charcoal black for the ink, so the application can tint it later. Must work as an exact tracing overlay for the original photos in each of the nine cells.
